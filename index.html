Electrical Safety

Quick safety tips every homeowner should know

A few simple habits go a long way in preventing electrical accidents at home.

01

Never overload a socket

Avoid plugging multiple high-wattage appliances into one extension board — it's a leading cause of overheating and fires.

02

Test your MCB monthly

Press the test button on your MCB/RCCB every month to confirm it trips correctly during a fault.

03

Keep water away from switches

Never touch switches or sockets with wet hands, and keep switchboards away from splash zones like sinks.

04

Don't ignore warm switches

A warm switchboard, burning smell, or flickering light is an early warning sign — get it checked before it becomes an emergency.

05

Use certified components only

Cheap, uncertified wires and MCBs may look the same but often lack proper insulation and current ratings.

06

Schedule an annual checkup

Have a licensed electrician inspect your wiring once a year, especially in older homes.
